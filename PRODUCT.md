## Platform

## Stack

delegated: Next.js (App Router) + React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + GSAP/ScrollTrigger + Framer Motion + Lenis — pinned by the homepage brief.

## Users

Primary: household customers in Pakistan who want fresh, trustworthy farm milk delivered to their door.
Secondary: milk distributors / gawalay evaluating a reliable farm source.
[Inferred from brief]

## Product Purpose

Al Shukr Dairy Farm sells farm-to-customer fresh milk. The customer-facing homepage exists to communicate purity, animal care, and reliable delivery — and to drive "Order Fresh Milk" / WhatsApp contact actions.
[Inferred from brief]

## Positioning

"Purity Promised" — milk quality begins with healthy cows and buffaloes, careful collection, quality control, and fresh delivery. Modern farm operations and traceability support trust without exposing internal systems.
[Confirmed from brief]

## Brand Commitments

- Official logo: circular black emblem with cow silhouette, gold/white milk splash, "Al Shukr Dairy" / "Purity Promised"
- Palette from logo: black, warm gold, cream/ivory, milk white; subtle olive/green accents
- Voice: premium, warm, trustworthy, authentic Pakistani dairy — not SaaS, ERP, supermarket, or children's brand
- Homepage only: no ERP, dashboard, auth, admin, livestock management, finance, or complex e-commerce

## Scope

Customer-facing homepage only. Technical/ops documents are reference for understanding the business, not features to build.
